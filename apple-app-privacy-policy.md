# Tall Grass - Apple App Privacy Details

App privacy summary for iOS submission:

- Bundle identifier: `com.dragonpixelstudio.tallgrass`
- Data types collected: none from user accounts (no login required).
- Data used for tracking: only if ad network feature flags are enabled.

## Data types declared for App Store Connect

- Contact Info: not collected
- Health & Fitness: not collected
- Financial Info: not collected
- Location: not collected
- Contacts: not collected
- Photos & Videos: not collected
- Audio Files: not collected
- Browsing History: not collected
- Search History: not collected
- User IDs: collected only if used internally for ad/service IDs
- Browsing ID / Device IDs: used through ad SDK behavior where required by ads platform
- Diagnostics: crash and performance signals may be collected by platform services.

## Advertising and IDFA/ATT

- If advertisements are enabled in the final app:
  - App Tracking Transparency messaging should match the platform policy.
  - The IDFA usage should be consistent with whether personalized ads are enabled.

## Third-party

- Google AdMob for ads.
- Crash/SDK telemetry only as required by platform and ad services.
