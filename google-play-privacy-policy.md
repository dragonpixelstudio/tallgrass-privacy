# Tall Grass - Google Play Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-14

## 1) Information we collect

Tall Grass is a casual game. We store game progress locally on the device using
capacitor preferences.

- Local game save key: `tallgrass_save_v2`
- Ad support IDs and device-level identifiers required by mobile ad SDKs
- Basic technical diagnostics for crash prevention

## 2) How we use data

- To keep your game state and settings
- To show and measure advertisements
- To provide app functionality and ad-related consent flows

## 3) Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Limited technical/ad-related data is processed by ad
providers under their privacy policies.

## 4) Advertising and consent

- Google AdMob is used for rewarded and interstitial ads.
- Users may manage privacy settings through the app if required by ad consent.

## 5) Your rights

You may request deletion or removal of your local data by clearing app storage in
device settings or uninstalling the app. We do not provide account-level data
portability for anonymous users.
